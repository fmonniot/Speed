Clear space

½× ½×

Give it room

Keep clear space equal to half the icon's width (½×) on every side, free of type, imagery, or other logos. The dashed boundary marks the minimum.

The corner module shows the unit: ½× is exactly the corner radius of the launcher tile, so the spacing always scales with the mark.

clear space = ½ × width

Minimum size

48 dplauncher
32 dplist / top bar
24 dpstatus bar · use favicon
16 pxfavicon · floor

Never render the mark below 16 px. At and below ~24 dp switch to the favicon build — its thicker stroke and larger hub keep the arc and needle from closing up. Above 32 dp the standard launcher is correct.

Misuse

The mark is a single, fixed relationship between arc, needle and hub. Don't redraw it.

✓Use the supplied artwork at its true proportions.
×Don't stretch or distort the proportions.
×Don't rotate or re‑angle the needle.
×Don't recolour outside the green‑seed palette.
×Don't add drop shadows, bevels or glows.
×Don't use low‑contrast tone‑on‑tone fills.
